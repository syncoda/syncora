Alice sat at her desk, staring at the blank page before her. The cursor blinked impatiently, waiting for her to type something, anything that might break the silence of her empty mind.

She had promised herself that today would be different. Today she would write something meaningful, something that would matter. But the words seemed to be hiding from her, locked away in some distant corner of her consciousness where she couldn't find them.

"Just start," she muttered to herself. "Start anywhere."

Her fingers hovered over the keyboard, trembling slightly with uncertainty and caffeine. The cold coffee beside her had become cold an hour ago, forgotten as she wrestled with her thoughts.

A sound from downstairs interrupted her concentration. Was that the mail carrier? She checked the clock. Yes, right on schedule. The world outside continued on, indifferent to her internal struggle.

Back to the blank page. Back to the cursor blinking like a tiny heartbeat. Back to the impossible task of finding the right words.

Maybe tomorrow would be better. Tomorrow she might find what she was looking for today.

She shut her laptop and decided to take a walk.
